# Play Store listing — Tiny Tunes Studio v1.0.0

**App name (≤30):** Tiny Tunes Studio: Kids Music

**Short description (≤80):** Toy piano, drums, guitar, beat maker and karaoke with silly voices for kids!

**Full description:**

Tiny Tunes Studio is a bright, friendly music studio made for little hands. Nine colourful rooms invite children to tap, strum, drum and sing – and nothing they do is ever "wrong".

🎹 PIANO, XYLOPHONE & MAGIC FLUTE
Eight big rainbow keys with animal friends, letters or Do-Re-Mi. Play with many fingers at once or slide across the keys for a glide.

🎸 GUITAR
Pick a chord, then swipe across the strings to strum – or tap one string at a time.

🥁 DRUMS & 🤪 SILLY SOUNDS
Big pads for boom, snap, clap and crash, plus boings, quacks, zaps and bubbles.

🎛️ BEAT MAKER
Start from a ready-made groove (Stomp, Dance, Parade, Bounce, Rain) and switch squares on and off while it loops. Three speeds: turtle, bunny and cheetah.

🎶 PLAY A SONG
The next key shines – press any key and the tune moves on, so every child can play Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, Mary Had a Little Lamb, Old MacDonald and more.

🎤 KARAOKE
Sing along with a backing track and bouncing lyrics, then hear yourself back as a Pop Star, in a Big Hall, as a Chipmunk, a Giant or a Robot!

FOR GROWN-UPS
• No login, no accounts, no chat, nothing to type
• The karaoke voice stays in the phone's memory and is never saved or sent; you can switch the microphone off
• Loudest-volume limit, key labels and gentle sparkles in a grown-ups area behind a parent gate
• Sparkles fade softly and never strobe
• One small, family-safe ad – only inside the grown-ups area, never on the play screens
• All sounds and music are made by us; the songs are traditional nursery songs
